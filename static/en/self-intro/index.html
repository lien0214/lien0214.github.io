07

Before, Service A called Service B over the network, which cost around 100 milliseconds per call. After the migration, both run in the same monorepo process and A simply calls B’s function. In practice this was hard for a few reasons. The monorepo had been designed for shared libraries, not for running backend services with their own startup lifecycle. We used several backend frameworks, and they interacted badly with the monorepo’s dependency resolution. There were workspace-level version conflicts that only showed up at runtime, without obvious stack traces. And nobody on my immediate team, including my mentor, had done this before.

03 · Research · IIS, Academia Sinica

Per-agent rotation in multi-agent RL

Jul – Oct 2026 · paper under review at AAMAS 2027

IIS logo

The idea

Agents that share one network tend to act alike, but teams need different roles.

Existing methods let each agent rescale shared features. I let each agent also rotate them, at only 6.7% more parameters.

7 / 8

SMAC maps end at or above the baseline, and most learn faster

Controls show the gain isn’t just extra parameters

Reported where it loses, too: randomized team compositions

Yi-Wei Lien · Backend & Systems Engineer

12

Briefly on research. This summer I did a research internship at IIS, Academia Sinica, and the work became a paper now under review at AAMAS 2027. The problem: in multi-agent reinforcement learning, agents usually share one network, which pushes them to behave alike even when the team needs different roles. Existing methods let each agent rescale the shared features. I added a per-agent rotation, so agents can also orient features differently, at 6.7 percent more parameters. On eight StarCraft benchmark maps it learns faster on most maps and ends at or above the baseline on seven. I also ran controls to prove the gain isn’t just extra parameters, and reported the setting where it loses. I bring up research not because I’m pivoting to ML, but because it’s the same habit as the GPU work: careful experiments and honest results.
